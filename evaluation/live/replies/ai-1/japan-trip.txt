For your 10-day Japan trip, I’d lean toward Oyado Koto no Yume as the better overall choice for two people. You’ll have three nights in Takayama, and staying close to the old town makes it easier to enjoy the historic streets, local markets, and a relaxed evening without spending too much of your budget on accommodation.

The main reason to choose Honjin Hiranoya Kachoan instead is if a more luxurious ryokan experience, especially a private onsen, is one of the highlights you want from Japan.

One important detail for both options: confirm that they can provide a fully vegetarian kaiseki dinner, including suitable broth and sauces, rather than assuming that removing meat and fish from a standard meal is enough.
