Good to know

  • You always press Send. Baton only types a draft. It never sends anything for you.
  • Saved on this device. Your batons stay in your browser. Want a backup? Press Sign in at the top of Baton.
  • Real numbers only. Usage comes from each AI's own pages. Where a service shows no limit, Baton counts your messages and says so.
  • Private suggestions. Baton picks the best AI for your task with a small model that runs in your browser.
