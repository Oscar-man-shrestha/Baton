How to use Baton

Everything it does, in plain words

➜ Pass your chat to another AI

Press the glowing button by Send, pick an AI, then press Send there.

✎ Say what's next

Type your next step in What's next? and press Enter.

◎ The right AI for the task

Baton suggests the best AI for your task that still has room left.

▮ See how much is left

The bar under the chat box shows your usage on that AI.

↻ Same AI, another account

Choose …, another account, switch accounts, then Continue your project here?

▤ Batons and folders

Drag a baton onto a folder, or press Select → Move to folder. Tap a folder to open it.

☁ Backups (optional)

Everything stays in this browser. Sign in to back up to Google.

× Hide what you don't need

Press × to hide. Bring it back under Show on AI chat pages.

Good to know

Built to stay on your side

  • You press Send. Baton types drafts. It never sends anything for you.
  • Saved on this device. Your batons stay in your browser unless you back them up.
  • Real numbers only. Usage comes from each AI's own pages. Baton never guesses a percentage.
  • No secret access. No cookies read, no private APIs, no passwords typed.
