How it works

Three moves. Ten seconds.

1

Press the glowing button by Send

On any supported AI chat, Baton adds a small glowing button next to the chat box, plus a usage bar showing how much you have left. You can hide either one with a single tap.

2

Pick where to continue

Baton reads your latest message and suggests the best AI for that kind of task: code, research, writing, maths, images or news. It also checks who still has room. Tap a task to re-rank if the guess is wrong.

3

Read it, then press Send

The next AI opens in a new tab with a summary of your project already typed in as a draft. Nothing is sent until you press Send. Your chat is also saved as a baton you can reuse later.

The right AI for the job

Code goes one way. Research goes another.

Each AI's free plan is good at different things. Baton suggests based on what you're doing, then checks who still has room. Try it:

    92%

    Right on prompts it had never seen. Baton guesses the task from your latest message with keyword rules plus a small AI model that runs inside your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

    Updated without an update

    When a model gets better at something, Baton's ranking list changes online and every copy picks it up within a day. You can also set your own order, and Baton learns from the AIs you pick.

    Privacy and safety

    Built to stay on your side.

    Baton only reads what each AI already shows you on screen. It works within each service's rules, not around them.

    You press Send

    Baton types a draft. It never sends a message for you, in any AI.

    Saved on your device

    Chats and batons stay in your browser. Google backup is optional and only uploads when you press Back up.

    No secret access

    No cookies read, no private APIs, no session tokens. Usage comes from each service's own pages.

    No account tricks

    Baton never switches or rotates accounts to dodge limits. Switching accounts happens in each service's own menu.

    On-device model

    The task guess runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere for it.

    Open source

    The code is on GitHub, so you can read exactly what it does.

    Get Baton

    On your browser in two minutes.

    Baton isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet. Until it is, install it from GitHub like this. It works in Brave, Chrome and Edge.

    Open Baton on GitHub

    1. Download the code

      On GitHub, press Code → Download ZIP, then unzip it.

    2. Build the extension

      In the unzipped folder, run npm install && npm run build

    3. Open your extensions page

      Go to brave://extensions (or chrome://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.

    4. Load Baton

      Press Load unpacked and choose the .output/chrome-mv3 folder. Open any AI chat and look for the glowing button.

    Questions

    Straight answers.

    Does Baton give me unlimited AI?

    No. It makes moving to another AI take seconds instead of minutes, so a limit stops being a dead end. It doesn't get around any service's limits.

    What's a “baton”?

    A short summary of your chat: the goal, what's done, what's next, and key details. Baton writes it from your conversation, and you can read and edit it before sending.

    What are folders for?

    Folders group your saved batons, for example School, Work or one project. Pick a folder when you save, and find everything together later.

    Can I hide the button or the usage bar?

    Yes. Tap × on the usage bar, or “Hide this button” in the menu. Turn them back on any time in Baton under “Show on AI chat pages”.

    Why is a number missing for some AIs?

    Some services don't show a usage limit anywhere. Rather than invent one, Baton shows how many messages you've sent, and learns the limit if the service tells you when you hit it.

    Do I need to sign in?

    No. Everything works without an account. Sign in with Google only if you want a backup you can restore in another browser.
